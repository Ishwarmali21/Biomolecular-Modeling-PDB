
# Bio-molecular Modeling using PDB Resources

## Overview
This project focuses on the structural analysis and visualization of various enzymes using Protein Data Bank (PDB) resources. The project explores enzyme architecture, active sites, ligand interactions, and structure-function relationships through 3D molecular visualization tools.

---

## Objectives
- Study 3D structures of enzymes
- Understand protein structural organization
- Analyze active sites and ligand binding
- Compare structural features of different enzymes

---

## Enzymes Studied
| Enzyme | PDB ID | Organism |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| Lysozyme | 1LYZ | Gallus gallus |
| Trypsin | 2PTN | Bos taurus |
| Catalase | 1DGF | Human |

---

## Tools & Resources
- RCSB Protein Data Bank (PDB)
- Mol* Viewer
- RasMol

---

## Methodology
1. Selection of enzymes
2. Retrieval of protein structures from PDB
3. Structural visualization and analysis
4. Identification of active sites and ligands
5. Comparative structural study

---

## Key Observations
- Enzymes showed unique folding patterns
- Active sites were clearly visible in 3D structures
- Ligand-binding interactions were identified
- Secondary structures such as alpha-helices and beta-sheets were observed

---

## Results
The project successfully demonstrated:
- Visualization of enzyme structures
- Active site analysis
- Structural comparison between enzymes
- Understanding of structure-function relationships
